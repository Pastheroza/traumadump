Sol · AI companion

Someone to talk to, any hour.

Sol is an AI who listens without judging and answers gently. Sol only knows the experiences and short bio you chose in TraumaMatch, and lets you lead.

It sounds like things are very heavy right now. If you’re thinking about ending your life or you’re in danger, please call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number, now. You deserve support from a real person.

Sol is an AI, not a therapist, and can get things wrong. What you write to Sol is sent to Masky, which uses Google Gemini to write Sol’s replies, and is stored on Masky in the TraumaDump owner’s account. Up to 50 messages a day. In crisis, call or text 988.
